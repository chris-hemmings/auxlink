Home Wi-Fi

    
Networks the Pi joins by itself when they're in range (then the page is at http://auxlink.local). While the setup Wi-Fi is on, new networks are saved and joined once setup Wi-Fi switches off.

    

    

      
      
    

    

  


  
XIAO firmware

    
Update the XIAO on its own: unplug its USB-C from the music device, hold its B button and plug it into a USB port on the Pi (the wires to the Pi can stay). Then flash the firmware file from the releases page, or the one that came with this AuxLink. Afterwards plug it back into the music device (Android asks once: tick Always).
